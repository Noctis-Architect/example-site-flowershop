Our Heritage & Craft

Sustainable Stems, Honest Floristry

Founded in Salt Lake City, Native Flower Company was born out of a desire to break away from sterile, mass-produced floral imports and reconnect with the wild elegance of the American West.

Operating out of our sunlit studio in the historic Milk Block district (430 E 900 S), we partner directly with Utah and Pacific Northwest growers. We never use single-use floral foam, and every vase is selected to be cherished for years to come.

Delivery Policies & Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our same-day deliveries, bloom guarantees, and studio pickups.
