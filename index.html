Studio & Process

Fresh Stems, Honest Floristry

Our studio focuses on fresh, seasonal cut flowers sourced directly from regional family growers.

We receive fresh stems every morning, condition each flower in cool spring water, and arrange without floral foam. From everyday dining tables to wedding receptions, our arrangements are made to arrive fresh and last.

Foam-Free Mechanics

Reusable wire armatures and clean water, zero floral foam.

Direct Sourcing

Stems conditioned in our studio within 24–48 hours of harvest.

Custom Styling

Each order is hand-assembled for shape, balance, and vase life.

Studio Pickup

Order online for curbside pickup at our workshop doors.
